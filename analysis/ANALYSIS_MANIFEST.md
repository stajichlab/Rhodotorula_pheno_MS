### phase_siderophore (rhodotorulic acid / iron-sequestration investigation)
- **Path**: `analysis/integrated_analysis/phase_siderophore/`
- **Scripts**: `analysis/scripts/siderophore_mass_remining.py`, `siderophore_presence_absence.py`, `siderophore_nrps_pfam_screen.py`
- **Status**: done — real ortholog call achieved and cross-referenced; the specific genotype/phenotype discordance found is not yet resolved
- **Summary**: PI-requested investigation of rhodotorulic acid and related siderophore chemistry. Mass search (idea1-style, 20 ppm) found 29 matches across 4 compounds x 4 adducts; best rhodotorulic acid candidate (row 2190, [M+NH4]+) is the highest-intensity match found. Strain-level presence (raw abundance >0) came back ~99% across all 17 species. **PI then supplied the real reference sequence** (`tmpin/RA_NRPS.fa`, from *R. kratochvilovae* Y14, antiSMASH-confirmed NRPS gene cluster) — ingested to `reference/RA_NRPS.fa`. `diamond blastp` against all 278 proteomes (`siderophore_nrps_diamond_search.py`, supersedes the coarse Pfam stand-in `siderophore_nrps_pfam_screen.py`) gave a clean bimodal identity distribution and confirmed the ortholog in 275/278 strains — a real, discriminating genome-side variable. Cross-referencing the 2 gene-negative *R. mucilaginosa* strains against MS presence: **the compound is still clearly present in both**, at abundances comparable to ortholog-positive controls — doesn't confirm the hypothesized gene-compound link. Both strains have below-average BUSCO completeness (one is the panel's lowest, 81.0% vs. 95.1% average), pointing to an assembly-artifact explanation, not yet confirmed. **PI decision**: strains with BUSCO<90% are excluded going forward, discordance attributed to incomplete assembly, no further pursuit as biology. Built a candidate-ortholog multifasta (265 strains + reference, BUSCO>=90 filter, `analysis/scripts/siderophore_nrps_build_multifasta.py`), aligned with `mafft --auto`, first-pass tree with `FastTree` — `outputs/RA_NRPS_candidates.{faa,aln.fa,tree.nwk}`. Gene-tree-vs-species-tree comparison (`siderophore_nrps_tree_species_comparison.py`) and a species-colored rendered figure (`siderophore_nrps_plot_tree.py`, `outputs/RA_NRPS_candidates.tree.{png,pdf}`, embedded in `RESULTS.md`): broadly consistent with vertical inheritance for well-resolved species; the 3 largest species come back as a resolution artifact (gene is nearly invariant — only 61 unique sequence patterns among 266); *R. evergladensis* DBVPG_7922 flagged as a genuine, unexplained branch-length outlier. This closes out the phylogenetic side of the investigation for now. See `RESULTS.md` for full detail and remaining open items (MS2-confirm the top MS candidate; tighten the MS presence threshold).
- **Note**: `outputs/bfd_proteomes.dmnd` (1.3GB diamond database, regenerable via `--rebuild-db`) is gitignored (`*.dmnd` added to `.gitignore`) — not committed.

### class_level_aggregation (Strategies 2+3: SIRIUS class aggregation + GSEA enrichment)
- **Path**: `analysis/class_level_aggregation/`
- **Script**: `analysis/scripts/class_level_association.py`
- **Status**: done (2026-08-17) — see `CLASS_LEVEL_AGGREGATION.md` in that directory
- **Summary**: second-pass analysis aggregating the 10,949 dedup features into SIRIUS chemical classes (NPC pathway, NPC class, ClassyFire class; ran only on the ~29% annotated subset) and testing class association with a\*/C\*/area via (strategy 2) class-mean z-score Spearman correlation and (strategy 3) GSEA-style KS enrichment ES, both against block-restricted (species-tree-clade) 500-permutation nulls with BH-FDR, a\*/C\* hard-gated on a fresh area decoy. **Result: null for both color axes** — 0-1 of 502 classes survive BH-FDR<0.05 (a\*: 1 NPC-pathway hit in supernatant; C\*: 0); top a\* signals are single-feature classes (n_members=1) that don't survive. The area decoy saturates (129/502 classes, incl. 43% of cell/NPC) — shown to be the *known* biomass/global-abundance artifact (spearman(mean z, area)=-0.30, p=3e-7), not a machine bug: a globally-shuffled true-null phenotype through the same pipeline gives the expected ~3-5% FPR, and per-species blocks barely reduce decoy hits (43%→41%), so the association is within-species scalar-abundance scaling. Confirms Phase 2's feature-level null and extends the project's 6-null consensus (now 7 with GSEA). No follow-up class signal; recommendation unchanged (targeted pigment validation > further untargeted aggregation).
